Porma / Formwork Calculator

Fajardo-based column/beam quantity formulas and lumber board-foot factors; site-specific slab joists, pako, and slant braces. Add results to Project Sheets.

Direct plywood form area + nails only. Select a geometry type for framing. Separate scaffold staging has no automatic plywood.

Phenolic Plywood / Pynolic

Fajardo reference: 1.20×2.40m plywood = 2.88m²/sheet. Your original 2.44m² rule is still selectable: under that rule, 2.44m² → 1 sheet and 2.5m² → 2 sheets. Modern 1.22×2.44m is 2.9768m² gross. Effective cut layouts may require more sheets.

Coco Lumber / Kahoy at Slant Braces

Column/beam frames: Fajardo 12mm plywood board-foot factors; 3/4 inch phenolic uses conservative 12mm proxy. Non-tabulated 2x4 and 3x3 sizes use extrapolation. Slab and footing joists use site-entered spacing. Scaffolding uses separate Fajardo flooring staging board-foot factors.

Columns/beams follow Fajardo sheet-based frame factors (not joist spacing input). Slab: joists every 40 cm across the width. Your separate slants: one station per 2m of applicable run, not a Fajardo bracing design. Scaffold staging uses the Fajardo flooring allowance; extra braces are not double-counted.

Pako para sa Porma

Fajardo Table 8-10 gives 0.055kg per 4×8 plywood sheet for 2d finishing nails in a particular fixing pattern. All other sizes use manual site nail rate; concrete nails are NOT covered by the Fajardo plywood rate.

About BuildCalc

BuildCalc is an all-in-one construction material calculator for workers, engineers, contractors, and homeowners. Enter your project measurements, get instant material estimates, and reduce waste before buying supplies.

Offline Ready

Everything runs in your browser. No internet required after first load.

Saved On Device

Your estimates are stored locally on your phone or computer — nothing leaves your device.
